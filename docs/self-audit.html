How to read this report

Mutation score is the share of generated mutants your suite detected. A mutant is a small, syntactically valid change to your code: a flipped comparison, a changed constant, a removed return value. If the suite still passes, the mutant survived, which means no test observed that behaviour.

Survivors are not automatically bugs. Some mutants are equivalent (the change cannot alter observable behaviour, such as changing the rounding precision of a value that is already exact). Treat survivors as questions, not verdicts. The hint on each card suggests the kind of assertion that would settle it.

Fang only mutates the lines in scope, runs the suite with fail-fast, and treats a timeout or a crash caused by the mutant as detection. A green baseline is required; a red suite cannot distinguish a surviving mutant from a pre-existing failure.
